# TidyTroupe privacy notice — review draft

Not ready for publication. Fill controller name, jurisdiction, postal address, contact route and effective date, and reconcile this notice with the final binary and integrations before release.

## Your household, on your device

TidyTroupe is designed to help adults and children share household chores. Core household records are stored locally on the device. Family members can use nicknames and illustrated avatars. Children do not need to provide real names, birth dates, email addresses or photographs to use a local profile.

Local records may include household name, nicknames, roles, avatar choices, chores, notes, assignments, routines, rewards, reported completions, adult approvals and progress history. Adults should avoid putting sensitive information in free-text notes. Everyone with an unlocked shared device may be able to view the child experience; the parent area uses a separate gate.

## Optional device features

Microphone input is optional and activated by a button. The app must obtain microphone permission first and show when listening. TidyTroupe does not intentionally save raw audio. The installed Android speech recognition service may process or transmit speech under its own provider terms; recognition availability and offline operation vary by device and language. Do not enable child microphone input until the provider, disclosures and applicable parental-consent requirements have been reviewed. Text and touch alternatives remain necessary.

Text-to-speech depends on installed speech engines/languages and may use network processing. Spoken child instructions are disabled by default and separately parent-enabled. Child microphone input is disabled in this build. Notifications are optional; chore titles can appear in the unlocked notification shade, with generic public lock-screen content. Voice, notifications and files request only the permissions needed for the chosen action.

## Sharing, purchases and backup

No advertising or public profiles are intended. No third-party behavioural analytics SDK is part of the product specification. Confirm the final dependency inventory before asserting these as release facts. Amazon's distribution wrapper and the store may communicate with Amazon and process account/purchase data under Amazon's terms. Purchase receipt verification may send receipt/user identifiers to a trusted verification service when configured; document the operator, purpose, retention and transfer safeguards before launch.

An export is a copy of household data chosen by an adult. JSON exports are plaintext. Optional password-protected backups encrypt the same data using AES-GCM and a separate password. Backups are portable; lost passwords cannot be recovered. Files saved outside the app are controlled by the selected destination and are not automatically deleted when app data is deleted. Keep exported files private. No cloud sync provider is configured. Android automatic backup and device transfer are excluded by app rules; manual exports remain under the adult’s control.

## Retention, deletion and your rights

Household records remain on the device until an adult deletes them, clears app storage or uninstalls the app, subject to the final OS backup configuration. Use household/profile deletion to remove local records. External exports and store records are separate. Provide an adult support route for questions about access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or complaints where these rights apply.

## Children and legal review

High privacy defaults, no public comparisons and minimal profile fields guide the design. Before release, complete a data-protection impact assessment and determine UK GDPR/GDPR lawful bases, UK children's-code applicability, COPPA obligations and Amazon policy disclosures for every distribution country. Document processors, international transfers, security incident response and retention schedules. Do not claim certification or legal compliance from this draft alone.
