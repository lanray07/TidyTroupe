Delete your data

Last updated: 27 October 2026

TidyTroupe does not require an online account. Adults can remove individual profiles or erase local household records without requesting an account closure.

Household records are stored on your device. We cannot locate them by nickname or remotely erase them. Use the controls below on each device where you use TidyTroupe.

Delete one family profile

  1. Open TidyTroupe and unlock the parent area using your parent PIN.
  2. Open Family and choose the profile's edit control.
  3. Select Delete and confirm the named profile.

This removes the profile and its linked completion and reward-redemption records. Previously assigned chores become unassigned. Other household records remain, including any free-text mentions of that nickname in task titles or notes; edit or delete those separately if needed.

Delete all household records

  1. Unlock the parent area.
  2. Open Settings → Privacy and your data.
  3. Select Delete household data and confirm the deletion warning.

This removes local profiles, chores, routines, rewards, completion history and reward-redemption history. Chore reminders are cancelled. The parent PIN and some local preferences remain, so you can reset the household without changing that PIN. Deletion is not a recoverable trash operation; an external backup may be used to restore its contents.

Completely reset the local installation

To remove retained PIN/settings and other local app records, use your device's app-storage controls: typically Settings → Apps → TidyTroupe → Storage → Clear storage/Clear data. On a Fire tablet the path may be Settings → Apps & Notifications → Manage All Applications → TidyTroupe → Storage. Names vary by device and Fire OS version. Clearing data resets the installation. You can also clear app data before uninstalling the app.

You do not need the parent PIN for the device's clear-data function, but your device may require its own administrator or unlock credentials.

Backups and other copies

Delete exported JSON files, encrypted backups and any shared or cloud copies separately using the service or file manager where you saved them. Local deletion cannot remove these copies. Restoring an old backup brings its included records back onto that device. Repeat local deletion on any other device where you imported a copy.

Request deletion of information sent to support

Email banksmi@mail.com with the subject TidyTroupe data deletion request. You can request deletion of some or all information you previously sent us without deleting your local household.

Describe the request and, if relevant, the earlier email conversation it concerns. Use the email address involved where practical. Do not send a parent PIN, child voice recording or household backup. We may need proportionate information to identify the correspondence. Information necessary to meet legal obligations may need to be retained; we will explain any applicable limitation in our response. This email route cannot remotely erase device-only records.

Store and speech-provider records

Deleting TidyTroupe data does not delete your Amazon account, Amazon store records, email-provider records or records retained by a speech service you chose to use. Contact the relevant provider for those records. The current development build does not have an enabled live Premium purchase offer.

For more detail, read the TidyTroupe privacy policy.
