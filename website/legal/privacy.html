Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 27 October 2026

TidyTroupe is a family chore and reward planner published by Lanray (GitHub: lanray07). For privacy questions or requests, contact banksmi@mail.com.

This notice describes the current development implementation. Core household features use local storage without an online account. Cloud household sync and live Premium purchasing are not enabled in this build.

Information on your device

Adults can create a household and profiles using nicknames, illustrated avatars and adult or child roles. Local records include household names, profile identifiers and preferences, chores and notes, assignments, schedules, routines, rewards, completions, approvals and reward redemptions. A parent PIN is stored as a salted password-derived verifier rather than plaintext.

We use these records to provide the household planner and its private progress and reward features. Core app functionality does not upload them to a developer-operated household server. Children do not need to supply real names, birth dates, email addresses, photographs, contacts or precise location. Avoid entering sensitive information in nicknames or notes. People using the same unlocked device may see household information; the parent area has a separate PIN gate.

Optional speech and notifications

Adult speech input is disabled by default. If enabled, it requires microphone permission and a push-to-talk action. The device's installed speech-recognition service receives the spoken command and may send audio or transcripts to its provider for processing. TidyTroupe does not intentionally save raw audio. Recognition availability, processing location and provider retention depend on the installed service and its privacy terms. Typed commands and touch controls remain available.

Speech recognition is disabled in child view. Optional spoken child instructions are separately parent-enabled. The text-to-speech engine may process task titles and notes over a network; its provider's practices apply. Do not enable these features if you do not want the selected speech provider to process that information.

Reminders are optional. An unlocked notification shade can show a chore title; public lock-screen content is generic. You can disable reminders or revoke microphone and notification permissions in the app or device settings.

Advertising, analytics and purchases

The current app has no advertising SDK, behavioural analytics, public profiles, open chat or social feed. It does not sell household profile records.

The Amazon Appstore and its SDK operate under Amazon's own terms. The current build has no configured live purchase offer or production receipt-verification endpoint. If verified purchases are enabled in a later release, app-specific Amazon account identifiers and receipt identifiers will be sent to the configured receipt-verification service, and verified entitlement information will be cached locally. Household nicknames and chore records are not part of that verification request. This notice and the store disclosures must be updated for the operator and practices of that service before it is enabled.

Exports, backups and sharing

An adult may choose to export or import household records through the system document picker. JSON exports are plaintext. Optional password-protected backups encrypt the exported records using AES-GCM with a key derived from a separate backup password. Lost backup passwords cannot be recovered.

Your selected file or cloud-storage provider may receive a file when you choose that destination. External copies remain under your control and are not deleted when you delete local app records. Keep backups private and remove copies you no longer need. Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are excluded by the app's backup rules. There is no configured household cloud-sync service.

Retention and deletion

Local household records remain until you remove a profile, delete household data, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Profile deletion removes that profile's linked completion and redemption records and unassigns its chores. Free-text references elsewhere are not automatically removed.

Deleting household data removes local profiles, chores, routines, rewards and activity history. The parent PIN and some local preferences remain for a household reset; use the device's clear-storage control for a complete local reset. Backups, support correspondence and Amazon store records are separate. See data-deletion instructions.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive the sender address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that correspondence to answer the request and support the app. Email is processed through the services used to send and receive it. Please do not include a parent PIN, children's voice recordings or household backups. Correspondence is kept while needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations; you may request deletion of information you have sent us.

Your choices and rights

You can use core features without enabling speech or notifications, manage local records through the parent area, and export or delete your household. Where applicable data-protection rights apply, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability and raise concerns with your data-protection authority. Contact banksmi@mail.com about information you have sent to us. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete household records held only on your device.

This website and changes

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising or submission forms. GitHub may process connection information under its privacy statement. This is separate from local app storage.

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the current notice.
